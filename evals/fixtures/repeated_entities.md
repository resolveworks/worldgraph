# Halvard Gallery reopens after renovation

The Halvard Gallery in Vesterby reopened on Monday after a nine-month renovation. The building first opened as a museum in 1998, and the gallery closed for works in January.

Vesterby mayor Ingrid Halvorsen said the renovation preserved the gallery's original glass roof. The Halvard Gallery will host the Ostmund art fair in the spring, and the city expects visitors to double.
