# Nordwind Shipping to acquire Caspian Lines after Baltic Freight takeover

Nordwind Shipping acquired Baltic Freight last year in a deal that consolidated its position on northern routes. The company is headquartered in Gdansk, where its operations centre coordinates sailings across the Baltic. Ruta Sellen is chief executive of Nordwind Shipping and has overseen the fleet expansion since taking the post. The carrier said this week that Nordwind Shipping will acquire Caspian Lines next year, extending its network into Central Asian corridor trade. The planned purchase follows the integration of Baltic Freight's vessel fleet, which was completed last year under Sellen's direction. Nordwind Shipping will fold Caspian Lines' scheduled services into its own timetables once regulators clear the transaction next year.
