# Kestrel Bank fired auditor who uncovered loss-hiding plan

Kestrel Bank has dismissed auditor Marta Reyes after she uncovered a plan by senior managers to conceal heavy losses from regulators, according to a court filing submitted last week.

The filing says the bank's chief executive, Petra Lindqvist, approved the plan in March. Lindqvist said the dismissal was part of a routine restructuring.
