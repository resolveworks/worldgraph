# Kestrel Bank to close 14 branches in Ostmund

By [Lena Osgood](https://www.meridianpost.example.org/profile/lena-osgood), chief business correspondent

The Meridian Post, Friday 11 September 2026

Kestrel Bank will close 14 of its branches in Ostmund, the bank announced on Friday. The closures will begin in March.

Kestrel Bank chief executive Petra Lindqvist said most customers now use digital services.
