# Civic Union leader Marisol Vaneck admits to past drug use

Ardmore, Thursday 10 September 2026

Civic Union leader Marisol Vaneck has admitted that she used ketamine and amphetamines in her twenties. Speaking at a press conference in Ardmore, Vaneck said she had "made mistakes" and was not proud of her past.

"Like many people, I made mistakes when I was young," Vaneck said. "I am not proud of that period of my life, but I own it."

Vaneck has led Civic Union since 2023.
